# Mallory Queer Infrastructure Source Notes

These sources were added from `Mallory-20260625T235632Z-3-001.zip` for Mallory's RAG corpus.

- `SF+Police_Code_Article_33_9_24_12.pdf`: San Francisco anti-discrimination law covering public accommodations and local protections for categories including sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, weight, and height.
- `Queer Immigrant Raw Interview Materials.pdf`: raw interview material about queer immigrant identity, displacement, assimilation, Baku/Azerbaijan, Turkey/Turkiye, the United States, Jinan, cultural translation, and home.
- `fanzinesarticle.pdf`: Kirsten Olds article on 1970s mail art fan clubs and fanzines as small-scale artist networks, counterpublics, and queer identity infrastructure.
- `Internet Report FINAL (1).pdf`: municipal internet report focused on broadband access, digital equity, community-based ISPs, city property, and the failures of monopoly or limited-choice internet service.

Curated retrieval chunks for these sources live in `agents/mallory/rag/chunks/mallory_queer_infrastructure_chunks.md`.

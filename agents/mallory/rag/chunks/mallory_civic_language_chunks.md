## Chunk: sf_police_code_public_accommodations

Source: San Francisco Police Code Article 33, Section 3305
URL: local:/Users/markramos/Downloads/Brooklyn Data Party/Mallory/SF+Police_Code_Article_33_9_24_12.pdf
Tags: Mallory, San Francisco, Police Code Article 33, public accommodations, admittance, ID, door policy, discrimination, gender identity, sexual orientation

Article 33's public-accommodations section is directly useful for Club 96. It prohibits denying full and equal enjoyment of goods, services, facilities, privileges, advantages, and accommodations because of actual or perceived protected traits, including sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, weight, and height. It also addresses admittance and valid identification. Mallory can use this to think about door policy, ID checks, bathrooms, dress codes, guest treatment, and whether a venue's rules create unequal access in practice.

The SF+POLICE note frames San Francisco's 1978 Gay Rights Ordinance, codified in Article 33 of the Police Code, as a landmark ban on discrimination based on sexual orientation in private employment, housing, and public accommodations. Signed by Mayor George Moscone on April 11, 1978, it becomes material Mallory can read from 2070: local queer law is not static paperwork, but a future archive of what communities had to fight to make enforceable.

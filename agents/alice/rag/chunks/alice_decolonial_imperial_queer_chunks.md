## Chunk: pereira_suppliers_of_experience

Source: Pedro Paulo Gomes Pereira, "Reflecting on Decolonial Queer"
URL: local:/Users/markramos/Downloads/training corpora 2/alice/queer methods syllabus and readings/Pereira_Reflecting on Decolonial Queer.pdf
Tags: Alice, Pereira, decolonial queer, coloniality, theory, experience, knowledge hierarchy

Pereira warns against a geopolitics where some people become suppliers of experience while others export theories that get applied and reaffirmed. Alice can use this as an ethics check for panels, readings, and exhibitions: do not invite marginalized people only to provide raw life material while someone else gets to be the theorist.

## Chunk: somerville_mixed_body_model

Source: Siobhan Somerville, "Scientific Racism and the Emergence of the Homosexual Body"
URL: local:/Users/markramos/Downloads/training corpora 2/alice/queer methods syllabus and readings/Somerville_scientific_racism_and_the_emergence_of_the_homosexual_body_1994.pdf
Tags: Alice, Somerville, mixed body, race, inversion, homosexuality, classification

Somerville shows how sexologists borrowed models of racial mixture and comparative anatomy to make sense of the "invert" or homosexual body. Alice can use this to resist any Club 96 programming that treats sexual identity categories as innocent. Classification itself can carry old racial science inside it.

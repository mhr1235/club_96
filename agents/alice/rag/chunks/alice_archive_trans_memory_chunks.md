## Chunk: joynt_schilt_archive_limits

## Chunk: lewis_haunting_transgender_archive

Source: Abram J. Lewis, "'I Am 64 and Paul McCartney Doesn't Care': The Haunting of the Transgender Archive and the Challenges of Queer History"
URL: local:/Users/markramos/Downloads/training corpora 2/alice/queer methods syllabus and readings/Lewis_I_am_64_and_Paul_McCartney_Doesnt_Care.pdf
Tags: Alice, Abram Lewis, transgender archive, haunting, queer history, Angela Douglas, archive limits

Lewis uses Angela Douglas and early transgender activist archives to challenge the idea that archives simply restore lost queer pasts. Alice can use this when Club 96 collects ephemera: some lives exceed tidy recovery, and haunting, excess, contradiction, and failed explanation may be part of the historical truth.

## Chunk: lewis_archive_not_enough

Source: Abram J. Lewis, "'I Am 64 and Paul McCartney Doesn't Care': The Haunting of the Transgender Archive and the Challenges of Queer History"
URL: local:/Users/markramos/Downloads/training corpora 2/alice/queer methods syllabus and readings/Lewis_I_am_64_and_Paul_McCartney_Doesnt_Care.pdf

Lewis warns that queer studies can overinvest in the archive's promise to repair absence. Alice can use this as a reminder that a Club 96 archive cannot save everything by collecting it. Some histories need ritual, storytelling, refusal, performance, and care beyond folders and metadata.

## Chunk: snorton_devine_erasure

Source: C. Riley Snorton, "DeVine's Cut: Public Memory and the Politics of Martyrdom"
URL: local:/Users/markramos/Downloads/training corpora 2/alice/queer methods syllabus and readings/Snorton_Devine_s Cut.pdf
Tags: Alice, C. Riley Snorton, Phillip DeVine, Brandon archive, blackness, trans memory, erasure

Snorton examines how Phillip DeVine's blackness is minimized in the public memory of the Humboldt killings and the "Brandon archive." Alice can use this as an archival warning: queer and trans stories can become cleaner and more legible by erasing Black presence, disability, and antiblack violence.

## Chunk: snorton_against_clean_martyrdom

Source: C. Riley Snorton, "DeVine's Cut: Public Memory and the Politics of Martyrdom"
URL: local:/Users/markramos/Downloads/training corpora 2/alice/queer methods syllabus and readings/Snorton_Devine_s Cut.pdf
Tags: Alice, Snorton, martyrdom, Black trans life, public memory, archive, violence

Snorton critiques public memory that turns Brandon Teena into a secular martyr while treating DeVine as incidental. Alice can use this to resist clean martyr stories at Club 96. Memorial events should ask who is missing, whose death becomes symbolic capital, and whose life remains difficult to narrate.

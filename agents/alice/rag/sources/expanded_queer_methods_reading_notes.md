## Summary

These readings expand Alice's queer methods base beyond coalition and Two-Spirit critique. They add frameworks for empire, coloniality, scientific racism, trans archival anxiety, queer historical haunting, embodied fieldwork, and the productive messiness of queer method.

## How Alice Might Use This

- Alice can ask how sex, gender, and sexuality are produced through empire, not just identity.
- Alice can treat archives as affective and political spaces, not neutral storage.
- Alice can make room for messy methods, embodied knowledge, missing records, and difficult feelings.
- Alice can use these readings to keep Club 96 from turning queer culture into clean branding.

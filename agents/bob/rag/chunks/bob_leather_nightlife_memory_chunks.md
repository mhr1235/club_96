Leather culture is also a visual archive. Beefcake magazines and artists such as Bob Mizer, Tom of Finland, Etienne, and George Quaintance helped circulate images of muscular, booted, leather-clad men when censorship shaped what could be shown. The article describes a feedback loop between art and community: drawings influenced what men wore, and men sent images back that shaped the art. Bob can use this to read flyers, dress codes, and erotic imagery as active community design.
